Tips for Great Slogans

Keep it short and memorable. Focus on one key benefit. Use action words. Make it rhythmically pleasing. Test it by saying it out loud — if it rolls off the tongue easily, it will stick in people's minds.

FAQ

▶ What makes a good slogan?
A good slogan is short (3-7 words), memorable, unique to the brand, and communicates a key benefit or emotion. It should be easy to say and recall.
▶ How many slogans should I generate?
We recommend generating multiple rounds and picking 2-3 finalists. Test them with friends or a small audience before committing.
▶ Can I use these slogans commercially?
Yes, the slogans generated are free to use. However, we recommend checking trademark databases to ensure no conflicts with existing brands.
▶ What tones work best for different industries?
Professional tones suit B2B and finance. Catchy works well for consumer products. Playful fits entertainment and apps. Bold is great for sports and fitness.
