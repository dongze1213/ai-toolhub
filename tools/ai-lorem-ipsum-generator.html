FAQ

▶ What is Lorem Ipsum?
Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used in printing, graphic design, and web development. It has been the industry standard dummy text since the 1500s, when an unknown printer scrambled a section of Cicero's text.
▶ Why do designers use Lorem Ipsum?
Lorem Ipsum allows designers to focus on visual elements like typography, layout, and spacing without being distracted by readable content. It closely mimics the distribution of letters in English.
